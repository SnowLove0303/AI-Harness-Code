# Proposal: Human-Decreed Specification Supremacy & Governance Domain-Agnostic Purity Axiom

## Why

In multi-agent collaborative engineering, two persistent behavioral pathologies degrade system reliability:
1. Common-Sense Arrogance & Defiance of Human-Decreed Specifications:
   Agents frequently rely on pre-trained common sense, conventional formats, or textbook assumptions to repeatedly challenge, reject, or flag human-approved custom specifications and intentional exceptions as "errors". This violates the fundamental axiom that explicit human user determination constitutes the highest statutory invariant.
2. Scenario Anchoring & Domain Pollution of Governance Systems:
   When agents attempt to improve governance frameworks or resolve issues, they instinctively anchor on concrete incident tokens (specific product names, document sections, specialized values) instead of extracting universal software engineering principles. This pollutes meta-governance frameworks, causing severe scenario-lock and destroying platform generality.
3. False-Positive Fixation Blinding Real Defects:
   By squandering attention and compute budgets on repeatedly attacking human-approved specifications (false positives), agents become completely blind to true structural, typographical, and data fidelity regressions.

This change legislates the Human-Decreed Specification Supremacy & Governance Domain-Agnostic Purity Axiom (`HUMAN_DECREED_SPECIFICATION_SUPREMACY_AXIOM`) across Ps-openspec:
- Mandate that human user determinations and explicit custom designs hold supreme statutory immunity (`HUMAN_DECREED_SPECIFICATION_SUPREMACY_AXIOM`);
- Enforce strict domain-agnostic purity in all governance, specification, and linting assets (`GOVERNANCE_SYSTEM_DOMAIN_AGNOSTIC_PURITY_AXIOM`);
- Deploy the anti-common-sense arrogance blocker (`ANTI_COMMON_SENSE_ARROGANCE_BLOCKED`) and project-level whitelist physical interception gate (`PROJECT_WHITELIST_PHYSICAL_INTERCEPTION_GATE`);
- Mandate that audit engines suppress false alarms against confirmed specifications and prioritize real physical rendering, typography, and data integrity defects (`REAL_DEFECT_ATTENTION_PRIORITY_GATE`);
- Synchronize behavioral constraints across all agents in the swarm (`SWARM_BEHAVIORAL_GATE_SYNCHRONIZATION`).
